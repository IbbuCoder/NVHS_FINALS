Share a NotebookLM notebook

Made a NotebookLM notebook from your class notes? Share it so other students can study with it too.

How to share

  1. Open your class notebook in NotebookLM.
  2. Click Share (top right).
  3. Set general access to “Anyone with the link” as Viewer.
  4. Copy the link and paste it into the form below.
  5. Only share notebooks made from your own notes, not teacher tests or answer keys, and keep names and personal info out.

Submit your notebook

Submissions are reviewed before they appear on the site.
