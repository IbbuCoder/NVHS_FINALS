Share a notebook

Made a NotebookLM notebook from your own class notes? Share it here. The site's owner will look at it and use it to help build the lessons for that class.

What's a NotebookLM? It's a free Google tool that holds a class's notes, slides and assignments in one place.

Why we ask: we use shared notebooks as the source material to build real lessons, flashcards and quizzes. The more classes people share, the faster they get filled in.

How to share

  1. Open your class notebook in NotebookLM.
  2. Tap Share (top right) and set general access to "Anyone with the link".
  3. Copy the link and paste it below.
  4. Only share notebooks made from your own notes. No teacher tests or answer keys, and keep names and personal info out.

Log in to see whether your notebook was used and to message the site's owner. You can also share without an account.
